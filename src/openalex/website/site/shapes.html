Formula and methodology

Log activity
Each year is log((papers + pseudocount) / corpus size). The pseudocount is 5% of that cluster’s peak yearly count, so a year with almost no papers is not a steep ramp in log space. A year with share zero uses the median corpus size of the years that have a positive share.
Topic adjustment
The reference for a topic is the median demeaned trajectory of clusters whose largest topic is that topic, when at least 15 clusters share it. Otherwise the reference is the median of every cluster. Each cluster is corrected by the share-weighted mix of those references. Weight that is not assigned to a topic stays on the global median.
Shape comparison
Flat, linear, and quadratic curves compete with a permanent step and a positive shock that decays exponentially. Every candidate includes an offset for 2023–2025 when the year grid contains years on both sides of 2023. The abrupt shape is kept only when its BIC beats the best smooth curve by more than 6 and the jump explains at least 15% of the variance beyond that curve.
Gallery scaling
Each curve is the topic-adjusted log activity after subtracting its mean and dividing by the larger of its standard deviation and 0.5. Abrupt classes are aligned on the fitted onset. The grey band marks 2023–2025, the years absorbed by the break offset. The black line is the class median.
